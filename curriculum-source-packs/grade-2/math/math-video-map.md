## Video Policy

1. **No video is assumed relevant or safe** until Victor manually reviews it
2. **Cross-strand reuse is flagged** — a video used for Addition should NOT be used for Fractions
3. **Every lesson needs its own approved video** — no sharing across topics
4. **Backup video URL required** — in case primary is removed from YouTube
5. **Child-safety check required** — no ads, no inappropriate content, Grade 2 appropriate
