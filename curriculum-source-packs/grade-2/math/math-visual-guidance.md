# Grade 2 Mathematics — Visual Guidance

**Purpose:** Tell the generator what kind of illustrations/SVGs to create for each topic.
**Rule:** Every visual must match the lesson topic. No generic placeholders.

---

## Number Patterns & Counting
- **Primary visual:** Number line (1-100)
- **Skip counting:** Highlighted number line with jumps (e.g., 5, 10, 15, 20 with arrows)
- **Missing numbers:** Number line with blanks to fill
- **Backward counting:** Number line with left-pointing arrows
- **DO NOT USE:** Generic shapes, unrelated objects

## Addition
- **Primary visual:** Groups of objects being joined (counters, dots, blocks)
- **Without regrouping:** Two groups → combined group (e.g., 23 + 15 = 38 shown as blocks)
- **With regrouping:** Base-ten blocks showing ones → tens carry
- **Number line:** Jumps forward from starting number
- **Word problems:** Real objects (fruits, books, children)
- **DO NOT USE:** Subtraction visuals, fractions visuals

## Subtraction
- **Primary visual:** Objects being crossed out or taken away
- **Without regrouping:** Start with group, cross out some, count remaining
- **With regrouping:** Base-ten blocks showing borrowing (breaking a ten into ones)
- **Number line:** Jumps backward from starting number
- **Comparison visuals:** Two groups side by side, "how many more?"
- **CRITICAL RULE:** Result must NEVER be negative. Minuend must be ≥ subtrahend.
- **DO NOT USE:** Addition visuals, negative numbers

## Multiplication
- **Primary visual:** Equal groups (e.g., 3 groups of 2 mangoes)
- **Arrays:** Rows and columns (e.g., 4 × 3 as 4 rows of 3)
- **Repeated addition:** 2 + 2 + 2 = 6 shown as three 2s
- **CRITICAL RULE:** Must show equal groups or arrays. NOT simple counting.
- **DO NOT USE:** Addition-only visuals, unequal groups

## Fractions
- **Primary visual:** Shapes divided into equal parts with shading
- **Halves:** Circle/rectangle divided into 2 equal parts, 1 shaded → "one half"
- **Quarters:** Circle/rectangle divided into 4 equal parts, 1 shaded → "one quarter"
- **Real objects:** Chapati cut in half, cake/pizza cut into 4 slices, chocolate bar divided into 4, paper folded
- **Equal vs unequal:** Side-by-side comparison — one shape with equal parts, one with unequal
- **Fair sharing visuals:** 2 children sharing 1 chapati, 4 children sharing 1 cake
- **Number line:** NOT appropriate for Grade 2 fractions
- **Fraction notation:** Always pair "1/2" with "one half" — never show notation alone
- **DO NOT USE:** Fraction arithmetic visuals, equivalent fractions, comparing fractions, number lines for fractions
- **SVG priority:** HIGH — Fractions needs clear, simple SVG diagrams more than video

## Measurement
- **Length:** Ruler, measuring tape, classroom objects (desk, book, pencil)
- **Mass:** Balance scale, weighing objects
- **Capacity:** Containers (bottles, cups), filling and comparing
- **Comparison:** Side-by-side objects (longer/shorter, heavier/lighter)
- **Non-standard units:** Hand spans, footsteps, bottle caps
- **Standard units:** Metre ruler, kilogram weights, litre bottles
- **DO NOT USE:** Abstract numbers without measurement context

## Time
- **Primary visual:** Clock face with hour and minute hands
- **O'clock:** Hour hand on number, minute hand on 12
- **Half past:** Hour hand between numbers, minute hand on 6
- **Daily routine:** Clock + activity (wake up at 7 o'clock, lunch at 12)
- **DO NOT USE:** Digital clocks only, 24-hour time, elapsed time calculations

- **Notes:** TODO: Victor to confirm denominations
- **Counting money:** Groups of coins/notes with total
- **Simple purchase:** Item price + money given = change
- **DO NOT USE:** Foreign currency, abstract numbers without money context

## Geometry & Patterns
- **Shapes:** Rectangle, circle, triangle, oval, square — as real objects
- **Lines:** Straight and curved lines in the environment
- **Sorting:** Groups of objects by shape, size, colour
- **Patterns:** Repeating sequences (shape, colour, size)
- **DO NOT USE:** Abstract shape definitions, 3D shapes (unless specified)

## Place Value
- **Primary visual:** Base-ten blocks (ones cubes, tens rods, hundreds flats)
- **Decomposition:** 64 = 6 tens + 4 ones shown as blocks
- **Place value chart:** Columns for hundreds, tens, ones
- **DO NOT USE:** Abstract numbers without place value representation

## Reading & Writing Numbers
- **Primary visual:** Number cards with symbols and words
- **Matching:** Symbol ↔ word matching
- **Number line:** Position of numbers on line
- **DO NOT USE:** Operations, abstract representations

## Word Problems & Application
- **Primary visual:** Real-life scene (market, home, school)
- **Kenyan context:** Market shopping, sharing food, counting animals
- **Step-by-step:** Visual showing each step of solving
- **DO NOT USE:** Abstract word problems without visual context

## Data Handling
- **Primary visual:** Pictographs, tally marks, simple tables
- **Counting:** Objects in categories, tally marks
- **Reading:** "How many children like mangoes?" from pictograph
- **DO NOT USE:** Complex graphs, bar charts (Grade 3+)

## Number Concept
- **Primary visual:** Concrete objects representing quantities
- **Representing:** Stones, sticks, bottle caps grouped to show numbers
- **Comparing:** Two groups side by side
- **Ordering:** Numbers on a number line
- **DO NOT USE:** Abstract number symbols without concrete representation
