## Proposed Child-Friendly Goals

1. **Halves Introduction:** "Today you will learn what a half is. A half means one of two equal parts."
2. **Quarters Introduction:** "Today you will learn what a quarter is. A quarter means one of four equal parts."
3. **Comparing:** "Today you will learn to tell the difference between a half and a quarter."
4. **Patterns:** "Today you will learn to make patterns using halves and quarters."
5. **Digital Games:** "Today you will practice fractions by playing a fun digital game."
6. **Practice & Application:** "Today you will practice everything you know about halves and quarters."
7. **Assessment:** "Today you will show what you know about fractions and think about what you learned."
8. **Halves in Real Life:** "Today you will find halves in everyday things like food and objects."
9. **Quarters in Real Life:** "Today you will find quarters in everyday things like food and objects."

## Proposed Examples

1. Chapati cut into 2 equal parts → each part is one half
2. Cake cut into 4 equal parts → each part is one quarter
3. Orange shared equally between 2 children → each gets one half
4. Paper folded into 2 equal parts → each part is one half
5. Paper folded into 4 equal parts → each part is one quarter
6. Pizza cut into 4 equal slices → each slice is one quarter
7. Chocolate bar divided into 4 equal pieces → each piece is one quarter
8. Circle divided into 2 equal parts, one shaded → shows 1/2
9. Rectangle divided into 4 equal parts, one shaded → shows 1/4

## Proposed Quick Check Templates (6)

### QC-H1: Identify Halves (Shape)
**Q:** "This circle is divided into 2 equal parts. One part is shaded. What fraction is shaded?"
**Options:** 1/2, 1/4, 1/3, 2/2
**Correct:** 1/2 (index 0)

### QC-H2: Identify Halves (Real Object)
**Q:** "A chapati is cut into 2 equal pieces. You get 1 piece. What fraction of the chapati do you have?"
**Options:** 1/4, 1/2, 1 whole, 2/2
**Correct:** 1/2 (index 1)

### QC-Q1: Identify Quarters (Shape)
**Q:** "This rectangle is divided into 4 equal parts. One part is shaded. What fraction is shaded?"
**Options:** 1/2, 1/3, 1/4, 4/4
**Correct:** 1/4 (index 2)

### QC-Q2: Identify Quarters (Real Object)
**Q:** "A cake is cut into 4 equal slices. You eat 1 slice. What fraction of the cake did you eat?"
**Options:** 1/2, 1/4, 1/3, 1 whole
**Correct:** 1/4 (index 1)

### QC-E1: Equal vs Unequal Parts
**Q:** "Which shape shows equal parts?"
**Options:** [Shape A: equal parts, Shape B-D: unequal parts]
**Correct:** Shape A (index 0)

### QC-F1: Fair Sharing
**Q:** "2 children want to share a chapati fairly. What fraction does each child get?"
**Options:** 1/4, 1/3, 1/2, 1 whole
**Correct:** 1/2 (index 2)

## Common Mistakes to Address

1. Unequal parts presented as fractions
2. Counting parts instead of naming fractions
3. Confusing halves and quarters (thinking 1/4 > 1/2 because 4 > 2)
4. Thinking "one part" means "whole thing"
5. Fraction arithmetic (beyond Grade 2)
6. Using fraction notation without saying the word
