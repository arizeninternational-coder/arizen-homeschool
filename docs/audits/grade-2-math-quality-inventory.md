## Priority Recommendation: 2-Week Focus

If we had only 2 weeks to improve Grade 2 Mathematics, the greatest educational impact would come from:

**FIRST PRIORITY: Fix the 7 RED lessons (complete rebuild)**
- These lessons have zero or near-zero content
- Students seeing these lessons get no educational value
- Estimated effort: 2-3 hours each = 14-21 hours total

**SECOND PRIORITY: Fix Quick Check quality across all 55 live lessons**
- 47 lessons have invalid Quick Checks (no correctIndex set)
- This affects assessment validity across the entire curriculum
- Estimated effort: 15 min per lesson = ~12 hours total

**THIRD PRIORITY: Resolve 128 duplicate lesson pairs**
- Many lessons are exact copies with different titles
- Creates confusion and wastes curriculum space
- Estimated effort: Delete/merge duplicates = ~4 hours

**TOTAL ESTIMATED EFFORT: 30-37 hours (just over 2 weeks at 2-3 hours/day)**
